# Setlist

A simple workout log that lives on your phone. Log sets live, build routines, and see your progress.
No account, no app store, no fee. Your workouts stay on your own device.

**Open it:** https://cafe100elm.github.io/setlist/

## Install it

- **iPhone / iPad:** open the link in Safari → tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome → tap ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

After that it opens like any other app and works without a signal.

## Your data

Everything you log is stored only on your device. Use **Settings → Save a backup file** to keep a copy
or move to a new phone. You can import a Setlist backup or a Hevy export (CSV).
